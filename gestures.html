Every gesture

This is what you get before changing anything: each one starts out following the trackpad setting it copies.

Gesture Does Follows
One finger, moveMove the cursorTracking speed, approximated
TapLeft clickTap to click
Two-finger tapRight clickSecondary click
Three-finger tapMiddle clickThree finger tap
Four-finger tapUnbound — yours to assignYour Mac has no four-finger tap
Two fingers, moveScrollDirection, inertia, horizontal scroll
Two fingers sidewaysBack / forwardSwipe between pages
Three fingers, left / rightSwitch full-screen appsSwipe between pages
Three fingers, up / downMission ControlMission Control
Four fingers, left / rightSwitch full-screen appsSwipe between full-screen apps
Four fingers, up / downMission Control / App windowsMission Control
Four fingers, pinch inLaunchpadLaunchpad
Five fingers, spreadShow DesktopShow Desktop
Press and hold, then moveDrag / select textAlways available

Reassign any of them

Each swipe direction is its own setting, from a list of whole sentences rather than a menu.

Change your mind mid-stroke

Lay a second finger down during a move and it becomes a scroll, exactly as on the trackpad.

Zoom steps, rotate can't

No public macOS API can synthesize a magnify or a rotation, so zoom is sent as ⌘+ / ⌘−.
