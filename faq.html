Questions

The ones that come up first, and the ones that come up at the worst moment.

Does my phone need an app? +

No. It opens a web page your computer serves — any modern phone or tablet browser, nothing to install or sign in to.

Can someone else on my Wi-Fi move my cursor? +

No — finding the port is not enough; every connection answers an HMAC challenge first. What isn't protected yet is confidentiality: the link is plain ws://.

The cursor doesn't move at all. +

Almost always Accessibility permission: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → enable PadRemote. It starts working the moment you tick the box. The phone says which of the two causes it is.

Why does macOS ask for Accessibility again after a rebuild? +

Ad-hoc signing ties the grant to that exact binary, so a rebuild is a different app to macOS — the box stays ticked and the app is still refused. install.sh clears it for you.

The phone says “this site can't be reached”. +

PadRemote serves the page itself, so check it is running in the menu bar. If curl -I http://localhost:8787/ answers 200, the problem is between the phone and the Mac — a different Wi-Fi, or client isolation.

It worked yesterday and now the QR doesn't. +

Your Mac's Wi-Fi address changed. Open Connect a device… and re-scan — your phone stays paired, only the address moved.

Scrolling goes the wrong way. +

Open Settings on the pad. “Matching your computer” means your Mac decides it; “match my computer” means this phone is overriding — tap to hand it back.

The pad says someone else is using this computer. +

Two devices are connected and taking turns. Just touch the pad — the cursor comes to you a third of a second after the other one lifts.

My iPhone never buzzes when a drag starts. +

It can't — iOS Safari has no vibration API. That is why the buzz is a bonus: the animation and the click sound always arrive.

Does it work on Windows or Linux? +

Not yet. Both backends are written and unit-tested in CI, but nobody has watched a cursor move on either.

Where do my settings live? +

~/Library/Application Support/PadRemote/config.json, hot-reloaded — edit it and feel the change within half a second.

Not here? Troubleshooting covers two dozen more, and an issue is always welcome.
